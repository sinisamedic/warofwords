GottaPlay / War of Words

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026 · Current development preview

This policy covers the War of Words Android game and its pages at gottaplay.net/wow. The publisher is GottaPlay. It does not cover other GottaPlay games or websites you reach through external links.

Pre-release notice: GottaPlay is verifying its support and privacy mailboxes. The contact and data-request service is not open yet. Do not send personal information until a verified contact is published here.

Information used by the game

InformationPurpose and handling
Local progress and preferencesCampaign progress, equipment, saved battles, scores, nickname, language and sound settings are saved on your device so you can continue playing.
Online player identityOnline features use a randomly assigned player identifier and authentication session through Supabase. No email registration is required in the current preview. Session credentials are stored on your device and used to authenticate online requests.
Daily Challenge activityRanked play sends the selected nickname, language, linking rule, attempt information, letter paths and move timings to the service. These are used to reconstruct and validate the result, enforce the daily attempt rules and display rankings.
Endless resultsWhen you choose to submit an Endless run, the service receives its identifier, nickname, score, wave reached, word count, duration, language and linking rule. Unsubmitted run records remain on the device.
Connection informationWebsite hosting and online service providers process requests including an IP address and technical request information to deliver the service and maintain security. Log and backup retention depends on their configuration.
Messages you send usIf you contact GottaPlay, the email address, message and any attachments you provide are used to respond and handle your request. Please do not send passwords, authentication tokens or unnecessary personal information.

What other players can see

Online rankings display nicknames and game results. Choose a nickname that does not reveal your real name or contact details. The leaderboard does not display your authentication credentials or internal player identifier. Reading online rankings also involves a request to the online service.

Service providers and sharing

Supabase provides authentication and database infrastructure for online play. The current project is configured in the Frankfurt region. Service operation and support may involve processing in other countries under the provider’s arrangements. See Supabase’s privacy policy. Website hosting and email providers process connection data or messages as needed to operate those services. GottaPlay does not sell the game’s player data.

Advertising and purchases

The current development preview does not integrate Google AdMob or another advertising SDK and has no in-app purchases. An optional rewarded advertisement for continuing an Endless run is under consideration; it is not a feature of this version. This policy will be updated before advertising is introduced to explain the SDK, data categories, purposes, sharing and privacy choices. No advertising consent is collected by this page.

This website and cookies

The supplied War of Words pages use locally hosted images and fonts. They do not set cookies, write to browser storage, run analytics, display ads or embed social-network players. Animations respect your device’s reduced-motion setting. Opening an external link takes you to that provider’s service and privacy practices. Hosting infrastructure may keep technical access logs.

Storage and deletion

Local game data stays on the device until it is cleared or the app is removed, subject to any device backup or restore settings. Clearing local data does not delete online results and can remove the session that identifies your online profile.

The current online preview has no scheduled automatic expiry for player profiles, submitted results or daily attempt records. They remain in the service until removed by its operator. A verified deletion request should cover the profile and associated game records; limited records may need to be retained for security or legal reasons. Any applicable exception and retention period must be explained when the request is handled.

Before the public release, GottaPlay will finalize its operational retention periods, hosting and email-provider details, and the data-request contact. This pre-release policy is not a statement that those launch steps are complete.

Your choices and requests

You can play Campaign and Daily Challenge practice offline, choose a non-identifying nickname, and decide whether to submit an Endless result. For access, correction or deletion requests, see Delete my data. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights such as restriction, objection, portability or a complaint to your data-protection authority.

Security

Online game requests use HTTPS and authenticated service access. No service can guarantee absolute security. Do not share your saved session files or authentication tokens. Access to a device may allow access to locally stored progress and session information.

Children and age suitability

The public release’s intended age groups and store content rating have not yet been finalized. GottaPlay will align the release, online features and any advertising with that audience before launch. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact GottaPlay through the verified privacy contact when available.

Changes and contact

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. Material changes to data practices will be communicated as appropriate before they take effect.
