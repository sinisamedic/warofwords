How can we help?

War of Words is currently in development for Android. This is the official GottaPlay support page for the game.

Pre-release notice: GottaPlay is verifying its support and privacy mailboxes. The contact and data-request service is not open yet. Do not send personal information until a verified contact is published here.

Contact GottaPlay

Game support: hello@gottaplay.net — not yet verified
Privacy requests: privacy@gottaplay.net — not yet verified

Reporting an issue

When the support mailbox opens, include your game version, phone model, Android version, selected language and a short description of what happened. A screenshot may help. Remove personal information from screenshots and never send passwords or session files.

Progress and online results

Saved progress is local to your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage can erase progress and your online session. Cloud save and cross-device account recovery are not part of the current preview.

Ranked Daily Challenges and online leaderboards require a connection. Campaign and Daily Challenge practice can be played offline. If a submission fails, follow the game’s retry option; a local result is not a confirmed online result.

Privacy or deletion

Read the privacy policy or visit Delete my data for the request procedure and the distinction between local progress and online records.

Official social channels

Our Instagram, TikTok and YouTube profiles are not live yet. Verified links will be published on the War of Words homepage. No social account is required to play the current preview.
